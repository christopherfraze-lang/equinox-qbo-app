Privacy policy

Effective November 24, 2026

Summary

Equinox is a private tool used by one person to keep his own books. It does not collect information from the public, does not sell or share data, and does not use tracking, analytics or advertising.

What Equinox accesses

With the owner's authorization, Equinox reads and writes accounting data in the owner's own QuickBooks Online company: the chart of accounts, classes and journal entries. It uses the QuickBooks accounting permission only.

Where data is kept

Equinox runs on the owner's own computer. QuickBooks access credentials are stored in the operating system's encrypted keychain. Property-management reports it processes stay in the owner's private storage. Nothing is sent to any service other than Intuit's QuickBooks API.

Sharing

Equinox does not share, sell or rent data to anyone.

Retention and deletion

The owner can disconnect Equinox from QuickBooks at any time, which ends its access immediately, and can delete its stored credentials from the keychain.

Changes

This policy may be updated. The effective date above shows when it last changed.
